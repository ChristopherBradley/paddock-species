# NLUM v7 (2021) per-commodity probability vs national 2024 crop-species map

Generated by `nlum_compare.py`. Aggregate only — sampled paddock centroids and probability-surface summaries, no site-level GRDC records.

## Scope and caveats

- NLUM gives a **probability surface per commodity**, not a hard classification — this checks whether our predicted classes sit in areas NLUM rates as probable for the matching commodity, not a confusion matrix.
- Commodity groups combine seasonal variants by taking the max probability (Oilseed = max(W_OILSEEDS, S_OILSEEDS), same pattern for Cereal/Legume) — NLUM splits winter/summer cropping, our classes do not.
- NLUM year is **2021** (v7 surfaces); our map is 2024 — same 3-year gap already flagged for the WorldCereal half of E9.
- Same 250,000-point sample (seed=0) as `WORLDCEREAL_COMPARISON.md`, so both halves of E9 describe the identical point set.

## Enrichment: median commodity probability relative to the Abstained baseline

The scale-invariant version of the table above — each cell is (median probability at our-class points) / (median probability at Abstained points) for that SAME commodity layer, so cross-commodity scale differences cancel out. >1 means our classified points sit in higher-probability territory for that commodity than a typical abstained point. **This still is not perfectly fair across commodities**: a commodity with a low Abstained-baseline (oilseed, 1.1%) mechanically produces larger ratios for a given absolute increase than one with a high baseline near its 0-100% ceiling (cereal, 35.1%) — so an oilseed ratio beating a cereal ratio in the same row is not by itself evidence of stronger agreement. Compare within a column (down), not the size of the diagonal entry against other cells in its own row.
