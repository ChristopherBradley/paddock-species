# Mapped crop composition vs ABS broadacre area, by SA2

> **SUPERSEDED, 2026-08-25 evening — do not quote the numbers below as a validation.**
> This ran on the 12 x 12 km demo boxes, a **1.56 % sample** of each SA2, and reported a canola
> share error of 4.6 points. The same script over the 102 km Riverina run — which *censuses*
> whole SA2s instead of sampling them — puts the error at three times that, with a consistent
> sign, and finds the map calling far more land crop than ABS says was sown. See
> **`ABS_COMPARISON_100km.md`**. The lesson is in that document: at 1.6 % coverage this report
> was measuring where the box landed, not what the map knows.
