`cat /scratch/xe2/cb8590/paddock-species-data/derived/orch/state.json`; between them they are the

Started 2026-09-12 by request: run the rest of the years the way 2024 was run at 9 km, expect

## 1. What is running

A single **PBS orchestrator job** (`my9km_orch`, `normalbw`, 1 CPU, 8 GB, 24 h, ~1.25 SU/h) drives
every stage of every year. It is a PBS job and not a login-node launcher on purpose: NCI sets
`KillUserProcesses=yes`, so a login-node loop dies with its SSH session — that is exactly how the
2024 launcher died twice on 2026-09-10. A compute node can `qsub` **and** see its own jobs in
`qstat` (verified 2026-09-12, job 178839997), which is what makes this possible. The job hands
over to a fresh copy of itself ~2 h before its walltime ends.

```
/scratch/xe2/cb8590/paddock-species-data/derived/orch/
  multiyear_9km.sh    the pass loop (everything is recomputed from disk each pass; killing it
                      mid-pass loses nothing, every run_national.sh stage is idempotent)
  multiyear_9km.pbs   the PBS wrapper
  config              sourced at the top of EVERY pass — retune live, no restart needed
  state.json          written each pass: progress, queue, SU, ETA          <-- read this first
  orch.log            one line per pass plus every submission
  ledger_<year>.txt   every PBS job id this run submitted for that year (basis of the SU figures)
  done_<year>         written when that year's GeoPackages are on /g/data
  STOP / HALT         see section 5
```

## 2. Year order and per-year pipeline

`2023 2022 2025 2021 2020 2019 2018 2017` — 2023 and 2022 first because ABS publishes sown area by
SA2 only for 2022–2024, so they are the two years that can still be independently scored; then
newest-first, because recent years have the denser Sentinel-2 record and so give the more
conservative early cost signal.

Per year, in `$D/national<YEAR>_9km`, all by `run_national.sh`:
`grid` (copies the 2024 9 km grid, relabelled) → `chunks` (60) → `presegment` (60 CPU jobs) →
`sampredict` (15 GPU jobs, SAM+predict fused) → `merge` → `boundary` → `overlaps` → `classified`
→ copy the three final GeoPackages to `/g/data/xe2/cb8590/paddock-species-national-9km/`.

Settings are exactly the adopted 2024 9 km configuration: `NCHUNK=60 PER_SAM=4`,
`PRESEG_EXTRA="--output-crs EPSG:3577"`, `SAM_EXTRA="--prompt-image-only --fp16"`,
`PS_MEM=8GB PS_WALLTIME=10h SP_WALLTIME=6h`, model `group3_map_sharma6.joblib`, **plus
`--no-zarr`** (section 4).

## 3. Cost control

Baseline: the 2024 9 km run cost **2,518 SU** over 75 jobs (re-derived 2026-09-12 from its own job
ledger; the 2,534 in `handoff.json` counts the pilot separately). Eight years ≈ **20.1 KSU**, which
is what was expected. `xe2` had 83.76 KSU of its 204 KSU quarterly grant left at launch.

The orchestrator halts submission on its own if cost runs away. Each pass it converts progress into
"year-equivalents" using the 2024 run's own cost split (presegment 16 %, SAM+predict 84 %), and
halts if billed SU exceeds **1.478 ×** what that much progress should have cost — which is exactly
“projected total above **30 KSU**”, the ceiling the user set on 2026-09-12 (“continue without flagging
as a decision so long as it keeps tracking below 30 KSU”). It was 1.35 (27.4 KSU) before that — a self-calibrating
guard that needs no forecast and cannot fire on noise (it is disabled below 0.3 year-equivalents).
`su_billed`, `su_expected_at_this_progress` and `su_projected_all_years` in `state.json` are the
numbers behind it. Every SU figure is **billed**, read from finished jobs' PBS epilogues; live jobs
count 0, so the number is always an understatement, never a forecast dressed up as a measurement.

## 4. The one deliberate change from the 2024 run: `--no-zarr`

Inodes, not terabytes and not SUs, were the binding constraint. A 9 km year writes ~420,000 files,
of which ~346,000 are the per-batch `.zarr` paddock time-series stores (124 files each × 3,993
batches). The `xe2` scratch **inode** quota had **749,000 free** at launch, so eight years with
zarrs (3.4 M files) would have run the project — and every other xe2 user — out of inodes during
the second year. With `--no-zarr` a year costs ~72,000 files, so eight years need ~576,000 and fit.

Nothing else changes: the `.gpkg` predictions, the merged map and every downstream product are
identical. A zarr can be rebuilt for any year by re-running predict over that year's composites,
which are kept. The orchestrator also halts if free inodes fall below 120,000.

**Open option, not yet taken:** the three retired 3 km runs (`national2022`, `national2023`,
`national2024`) hold ~1.2 M files between them and are superseded by the 9 km map (user decision
2026-09-11 to stay with 9 km tiling). Deleting them would roughly triple the inode headroom. Not
done — it is irreversible and nobody has asked for it.

## 5. Controls

| I want to | Do this |
|---|---|
| see where it's up | `cat $ORCH/state.json` (`ORCH=/scratch/xe2/cb8590/paddock-species-data/derived/orch`) |
| see what it did | `tail -40 $ORCH/orch.log` |
| stop it cleanly | `touch $ORCH/STOP` — exits next pass; live PBS jobs finish |
| stop it submitting but keep it reporting | `touch $ORCH/HALT` |
| resume after a halt | `rm $ORCH/HALT` (fix the cause first) |
| change job ceilings / year list | edit `$ORCH/config` — picked up on the next pass |
| restart it after it died | `qsub $ORCH/multiyear_9km.pbs` (safe at any time — but only ONE may run) |

**Only one orchestrator may run at a time.** Check with
`qstat -u cb8590 | grep my9km_orch` before submitting another.

Two ceilings, and they are not the same thing. `MAXQ=190` is the total-jobs-in-queue ceiling (PBS
starts refusing dependency releases near its 200-job per-user threshold). `PSCAP` caps *concurrent
presegment jobs only*, because the ceiling that matters for that stage is the shared DEA datacube
connection pooler, not PBS: 320 presegment jobs at once once exhausted it and **79 % of tiles
failed while every PBS job still exited 0**. It starts at the proven-safe 64 and is raised in steps
only after checking job logs for pooler errors.
