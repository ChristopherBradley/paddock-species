# Fields of the World against SAM boundaries, over the 2024 NVT trials

Generated by `src/paddocks/ftw_compare.py` on 2026-09-12. Aggregate only: no trial codes, no coordinates.

- sites: **557** NVT trials sown in 2024
- AOI: 1000 m box around each trial point (399 ha)
- FTW: `label == field`, year 2024, 4283 polygons over these AOIs
- SAM: `national_2024_crops_final.gpkg`, 984307 polygons over these AOIs
- retention filter applied to both: 5-300 ha and compactness <= 8

## 1. Does the source give this trial a paddock?

| | FTW | SAM |
|---|---|---|
| trial point inside a polygon | 90.7% | 62.5% |
| inside a polygon that passes the filter | 49.7% | 55.7% |

The second row is the share of trials that would yield a usable labelled paddock if the pipeline were built on that source. **It is not a clean head-to-head.** The released SAM map has already had this filter applied during production, so almost nothing in it fails on shape (0.15% of polygons exceed compactness 8, and those come from the later boundary merge), while FTW is compared raw. Every row of this section flatters SAM by that much. The comparisons that do not depend on SAM's pre-filtering are the area distribution and the IoU in section 2, because the map retains polygons across the whole 5-300 ha range and beyond (sub-5 ha and over-300 ha polygons are both present in it).

## 2. How big is the polygon it gives?

On the **321** trials where both sources contain the point:

| statistic | FTW | SAM |
|---|---|---|
| median area (ha) | 109.2 | 54.7 |
| mean area (ha) | 459.3 | 85.8 |
| 90th percentile (ha) | 825.3 | 221.7 |
| median compactness | 6.76 | 5.47 |

- median FTW/SAM area ratio **1.49x** (FTW larger on 73% of trials)
- FTW at least 3x larger on **32.4%** of trials, at least 10x larger on **17.1%**
- median IoU between the two containing polygons **0.53**
- IoU below 0.5 on **45.5%** of trials

A high area ratio with a low IoU is the merged-paddock failure the single-site check found: FTW returns one polygon covering the trial paddock and its neighbours.
